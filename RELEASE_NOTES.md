Mouse Rate 1.0.3 adds the leaderboard.

**New:** the report asks if you want to share your results on the leaderboard at mouse.almostperfecttech.com. It's optional. The button opens the leaderboard in your browser with your results filled in. Add a nickname or get a made-up one, and choose whether to show your mouse's name. Nothing is shared until you press Share on the website.

**Still no network code.** The app itself never sends anything. Sharing happens in your browser, and only when you choose to.

**New:** a Leaderboard link at the bottom of the window.

**Also in this version:** Copy and paste in the YouTube comments opens the mouse video (from 1.0.2), and Mouse Rate clears its own old Input Monitoring entry and asks once (from 1.0.1).

**Updating from an earlier copy:** macOS treats each new version as a new app, so it asks for Input Monitoring one more time. Turn the switch on, then click Quit & Reopen.

**Install:** open the DMG and drag Mouse Rate into Applications. The first time you open it, macOS says it can't verify the app because it isn't signed with a paid Apple certificate yet. Go to System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway. When Mouse Rate asks for Input Monitoring, click Open System Settings, turn on Mouse Rate, then click Quit & Reopen.

No network code. Nothing is saved or sent by the app. Built from this exact tag by GitHub Actions. The SHA-256 checksum is attached.
